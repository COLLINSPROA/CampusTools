# Licensing and service notes

CampusTools starter code is provided as a project scaffold. Third-party packages and models have their own licenses and terms.

In particular, the `@imgly/background-removal` dependency has licensing conditions (including AGPL/commercial licensing considerations). Review the exact current package license and model terms before deploying a public or commercial service. If those terms are unsuitable, replace the background-removal adapter with a model and runtime whose license fits your intended use.

Supabase free-plan limits and terms can change. Review current pricing and acceptable-use policies before launch.
